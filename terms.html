Passenger & Commercial Transit Conditions Across Saudi Arabia

1. Reservations & Bookings

Reservations confirmed via WhatsApp or signed corporate proposals are binding. Vehicles are allocated according to passenger count and schedule requested.

2. Chauffeur & Safety Regulations

All vehicles are operated by certified chauffeurs adhering to Saudi Ministry of Transport and Public Security road safety laws. Maximum speed limits and passenger seatbelt rules must be observed.

3. Cancellations & Adjustments

For daily charters, cancellations made at least 12 hours prior to scheduled pickup incur zero cancellation fee. Long-term corporate agreements follow specific contract clauses.

4. Support & Inquiries

For questions regarding terms, contact our dispatch operations team at info@allmadenatransportation.com or WhatsApp +966 51 102 4545.
