Privacy Policy

How All Madena Transportation Services Protects and Respects Your Data

1. Information We Collect

When you reach out via our online booking form, phone, or WhatsApp, we collect basic contact information including your name, telephone number, email address, pickup and drop-off points, and dates required to arrange your transportation.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your details are strictly utilized to coordinate vehicle reservations, assign certified drivers, prepare corporate invoices, and provide dispatch status updates. We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

3. WhatsApp and Communications

Communications made through WhatsApp are secured end-to-end according to WhatsApp and Meta policies. You can opt out of promotional messages at any time by messaging STOP.
